About the Resort

Your Own Private Paradise in Pampanga

Cubo Angelo Resort is a beautifully designed private resort nestled in the peaceful landscapes of Porac, Pampanga. Whether you're celebrating a milestone birthday, hosting a family reunion, or simply escaping the city with your barkada — we offer a serene, intimate setting that feels like your own personal haven.

🔒

Fully Private Booking

The entire resort is yours — no strangers, just your group enjoying quality time together.

🌿

Nature-Inspired Ambiance

Lush gardens, fresh air, and the tranquil sounds of nature create a truly relaxing retreat.

🎉

Perfect for Any Occasion

Birthdays, baptisms, team outings, or quiet family weekends — we're set up for it all.

How to Get Here

Cubo Angelo Resort is located in the lush, quiet town of Porac in Pampanga — easily accessible from Metro Manila via the NLEX, approximately 1.5 to 2 hours away.

📍
Address Porac, Pampanga, Philippines
🚗
Via Private Car NLEX → Dau Exit → MacArthur Highway → Porac direction. Specific address provided upon booking confirmation.
🚌
Via Public Transport Take a bus to Dau Terminal (Pampanga), then hire a tricycle or jeepney to Porac.
⏱️
Travel Time from Manila Approximately 1.5 – 2 hours depending on traffic.
